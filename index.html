03 / I create experiences

Experience

SILENT is my tech startup and event company built on silent disco: wireless headphones, several music channels and new event formats where everyone chooses their own sound.

Discover SILENT →
About the project

Be different. Be together.

SILENT is my love of visuals, music and feeling, turned into an experience that stays with people.

Here togetherness starts not with sameness but with free choice. Every guest picks their own channel and music, yet stays part of a shared moment: laughing, dancing and meeting the eyes of the people nearby.

Project values

  • We start not with equipment but with what people should feel. So we look for new ways to make familiar event formats more engaging: with interactivity, play and unexpected moments.

  • We respect different tastes. Every guest picks their own channel and way of taking part, and togetherness doesn’t ask everyone to listen or have fun the same way.

  • Careful organisation, clear agreements and attention to the client’s needs matter to us as much as the technology itself. Guests may not notice these details, but they are what hold the event together.

  • An unusual format shouldn’t mean complicated organisation. We set up the equipment and show guests how everything works, so they quickly feel part of the event.

Our vision is an event culture where technology opens more room for interaction, creativity and freedom of choice, and people can be different while staying together.

Who it is for

One technology. Dozens of formats.

SILENT fits wherever people want to be together, each in their own way.

We bring everything and handle the organisation: choosing the format, delivery, setup and on-site support. You simply stay with your guests.

About

Max Vitvitskiy

ArtCreativityTechnologyMusicSpiritualityInspiration

I’m a Ukrainian visual artist working with photography and digital composition.

I create symbolic, surreal images that explore moments of presence — those instants when a person feels both small before the greatness of the world and deeply connected to something larger than themselves.

Flowers, animals and the human figure recur throughout my work. For me they are images of fragility and strength, of life and the search for meaning. Through them I explore the bond between the temporary and the eternal, the visible and the invisible.

I’m drawn to art that slows the gaze and brings attention back to what truly matters. My work is not about decoration but about presence. It is an invitation to pause, breathe and feel.

Faith is an important part of my life and naturally shapes how I see the world and how I create. I don’t try to illustrate it literally — rather, it remains a quiet foundation running through my work.

All my works are made by hand in a digital environment, without the use of artificial intelligence. Each piece is carefully built as a complete composition and released as a physical limited-edition work.

Beyond my own art practice, I explore creativity as a way of knowing oneself, create experiences for people and share what helps others unlock their creative potential.

I live and work in Ukraine, continuing my creative practice during the full-scale war.
