03 / I create experiences

Experience

SILENT is my tech startup and event company built on silent disco: wireless headphones, several music channels and new event formats where everyone chooses their own sound. We choose the format, bring and set up the equipment and support the event on site — from corporate events and weddings to school parties, festivals and wellness events, wherever togetherness and freedom of choice matter.

Discover SILENT →
About the project

Be different. Be together.

SILENT is a project in which I brought together my interest in visual culture, music and human experiences.

Here everyone chooses their own music yet remains part of a shared moment. We can be different without losing the sense of unity.

Project values

  • We start with what people should feel. That is why we create formats that bring interactivity, play and unexpected moments to familiar events.

  • Everyone chooses their own channel and way of joining in. It matters to us that people can stay themselves while sharing a common experience.

  • From agreements to technical preparation — we take care of the details, so organising the event is clear for the client and taking part is comfortable for guests.

  • We set up the equipment and explain how everything works, so guests can quickly find their way and join the event without unnecessary hassle.

We see the future of events in greater freedom of interaction, creativity and choice — where being different doesn’t keep people from being together.

About

Max Vitvitskiy

ArtCreativityTechnologyMusicSpiritualityInspiration

I’m a Ukrainian visual artist working with photography and digital composition.

I create symbolic, surreal images that explore moments of presence — those instants when a person feels both small before the greatness of the world and deeply connected to something larger than themselves.

Flowers, animals and the human figure recur throughout my work. For me they are images of fragility and strength, of life and the search for meaning. Through them I explore the bond between the temporary and the eternal, the visible and the invisible.

I’m drawn to art that slows the gaze and brings attention back to what truly matters. My work is not about decoration but about presence. It is an invitation to pause, breathe and feel.

Faith is an important part of my life and naturally shapes how I see the world and how I create. I don’t try to illustrate it literally — rather, it remains a quiet foundation running through my work.

All my works are made by hand in a digital environment, without the use of artificial intelligence. Each piece is carefully built as a complete composition and released as a physical limited-edition work.

Beyond my own art practice, I explore creativity as a way of knowing oneself, create experiences for people and share what helps others unlock their creative potential.

I live and work in Ukraine, continuing my creative practice during the full-scale war.

Max V.
