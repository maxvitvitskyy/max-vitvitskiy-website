Speaking, trainings, masterclasses

Creativity can be felt, but it can also be learned, understood and developed. I share my experience with artists, designers and everyone creative who looks for inspiration and wants to step beyond template thinking. My goal is not only to pass on knowledge, but to light a fire and help unlock creative potential.

Discover SILENT →
About the project

Be different. Be together.

SILENT is my love of visuals, music and feeling, turned into an experience that stays with people.

Here togetherness starts not with sameness but with free choice. Every guest picks their own channel and music, yet stays part of a shared moment: laughing, dancing and meeting the eyes of the people nearby.

  • 01Experience comes first
  • 02Freedom to be yourself
  • 03Care in the details
  • 04Easy to join

Our vision is an event culture where technology opens more room for interaction, creativity and freedom of choice, and people can be different while staying together.

What it is for

One technology. Dozens of formats.

SILENT fits wherever people want to be together, each in their own way.

We bring everything and handle the organisation: choosing the format, delivery, setup and on-site support. You simply stay with your guests.

  • BusinessCorporate events, conferences, brand activations
  • CelebrationsBirthdays, weddings, graduations, hen parties
  • Kids & schoolsSchool parties, camps, youth meetups
  • Education & cultureMuseums, workshops, concerts, shows
  • Sport & wellnessYoga, runs, meditation
  • CommunitiesFestivals, markets, inclusive events

Tell us your event idea

We will find the format together. From 40 guests, setup takes about 30 minutes.
