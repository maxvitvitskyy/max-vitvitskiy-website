Manifest Yourself

AvailableEdition of 10

A powerful woman musician in a whirl of fabric, wild energy and cinematic movement: a visual expression of courage, creation and bringing an idea to life. A bold accent that fills an interior, studio or living room with passion and creative energy.

“The limits of what you can manifest depend only on you. Whatever you can do or dream, begin it. Boldness has genius, power and magic in it. Begin now.”

There is a moment before everything changes: nothing is certain, but something inside says move. This work lives in exactly that moment.

The woman is immersed in music: she does not just play, she becomes it. The cello here is not an instrument but her voice. The explosion of colour, fabric and energy around her is what happens when creation is no longer held back. Beside her stand leopards, not danger but strength, a primal untamed energy within.

Manifestation begins not when everything is ready but at the moment you decide not to stay where you are any longer.

The only real limit is the one we refuse to cross. Sometimes everything changes the moment you simply begin.
